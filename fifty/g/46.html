Before you switch it on

  1. Look. Every joint is a shiny cone. No solder touches a neighbouring pad. No stray strands of wire.
  2. These must beep. Probe the two ends of every connection.
    GNDSWD probe GND.1 · PineTime GND
    SWCLKSWD probe GP2 · PineTime SWDCLK
    SWDIOSWD probe GP3 · PineTime SWDIO
  3. Plug in. Nothing gets warm, nothing smells. If something does, unplug at once and go back to step 2.
  4. Flash the bring-up test. It reports every part it can find before you load the real firmware.pio run -e bringup -t upload && pio device monitor

Takt is built

Takt is the bench kit for writing your own InfiniTime app on a PINE64 PineTime, starting with a work-rest interval timer. The part you build is the safety net: a Raspberry Pi Pico in a printed pod running Raspberry Pi Debugprobe, with three pogo-tipped leads for the watch SWD holes, so a bad flash or a broken bootloader can be put right instead of ending the watch.
