A MeshCore LoRa repeater you solder yourself

Heat the pad and the pin together for two seconds. Feed solder into the joint, not onto the iron. Take the solder away first, then the iron.

Strip 3 mm, twist the strands, and tin the end before you solder it to a pad.

Unplug USB and disconnect any battery before you solder or change a wire. Work on a surface that does not burn, and wash your hands after handling solder.

Before you switch it on

  1. Look. Every joint is a shiny cone. No solder touches a neighbouring pad. No stray strands of wire.
  2. These must not beep. Set the multimeter to continuity and probe each pair. A beep means a short: find it before you plug in.
    3V3GND
  3. These must beep. Probe the two ends of every connection.

Varde is built

Varde is the dedicated repeater in a small private MeshCore network: two ready-made companion radios talk to phones over Bluetooth, and Varde sits high up in a window or attic and relays their messages where they cannot hear each other. It is an ESP32-C3 and an SX1262 radio module in a printed case with an 868 MHz antenna, powered from a USB charger.
