Before you switch it on

  1. Look. Every joint is a shiny cone. No solder touches a neighbouring pad. No stray strands of wire.
  2. These must not beep. Set the multimeter to continuity and probe each pair. A beep means a short: find it before you plug in.

Vegg is built

Vegg is a 2.8 inch touch panel for the wall or a shelf that runs openHASP: pages of buttons and sliders for lights, rooms, media and scenes. It talks to Home Assistant (or any automation server) over MQTT, so switches on the panel follow the real devices when they are changed elsewhere. One ESP32 board, one display module, twelve wires and a printed case.
