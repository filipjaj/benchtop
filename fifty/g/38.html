Before you switch it on

  1. Look. Every joint is a shiny cone. No solder touches a neighbouring pad. No stray strands of wire.
  2. These must not beep. Set the multimeter to continuity and probe each pair. A beep means a short: find it before you plug in.

Dreie is built

Dreie is the control and telemetry console for a bench brushless-motor test rig built around an assembled VESC controller. A knob sets motor current or speed inside hard caps, a stop button drops the motor to zero at once, a small display shows voltage, current, rpm and temperatures, and every reading streams to the laptop as CSV. The VESC, motor, supply, fuse and guarded fixture are bought parts wired on the power side; this package builds the console and its link to the VESC.
