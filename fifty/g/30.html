Before you switch it on

  1. Look. Every joint is a shiny cone. No solder touches a neighbouring pad. No stray strands of wire.
  2. These must not beep. Set the multimeter to continuity and probe each pair. A beep means a short: find it before you plug in.
    VMOT_12VGND
  3. These must beep. Probe the two ends of every connection.
    VMOT_12V12 V jack + · Grbl controller +
    GND12 V jack - · Grbl controller -
    LIMIT_XGrbl controller X- · X home switch NO
    GNDGrbl controller GX · X home switch C
    LIMIT_YGrbl controller Y- · Y home switch NO
    GNDGrbl controller GY · Y home switch C
    LIMIT_ZGrbl controller SpnEn · Z home switch NO
    GNDGrbl controller GZ · Z home switch C
  4. Plug in. Nothing gets warm, nothing smells. If something does, unplug at once and go back to step 2.
  5. Flash the bring-up test. It reports every part it can find before you load the real firmware.pio run -e bringup -t upload && pio device monitor

Strek is built

Strek is the controller box for a three-axis pen plotter: an Arduino Uno running stock Grbl 1.1, a CNC Shield V3 with three A4988 drivers, belts on X and Y and a small lead-screw Z axis that lifts the pen. It plugs into a 12 V brick and a computer, homes itself on three microswitches, and draws whatever G-code a sender streams to it. This package covers the controller, its wiring and its case; the frame and motion hardware are yours to build.
