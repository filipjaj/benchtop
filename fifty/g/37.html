Before you switch it on

  1. Look. Every joint is a shiny cone. No solder touches a neighbouring pad. No stray strands of wire.
  2. These must not beep. Set the multimeter to continuity and probe each pair. A beep means a short: find it before you plug in.
    SERVO_5VGND
  3. These must beep. Probe the two ends of every connection.
    SERVO_5VServo power VBUS · Capacitor + · Pan servo 5V · Tilt servo 5V
    GNDServo power GND · Capacitor - · Pan servo GND · Tilt servo GND · Core2 GND
    SERVO_PANCore2 G33 · Pan servo SIG
    SERVO_TILTCore2 G32 · Tilt servo SIG
  4. Plug in. Nothing gets warm, nothing smells. If something does, unplug at once and go back to step 2.
  5. Flash the bring-up test. It reports every part it can find before you load the real firmware.pio run -e bringup -t upload && pio device monitor

Stack-chan is built

A hobbyist build of the open-source Stack-chan robot: an M5Stack Core2 is the face, two SG90 servos turn and tilt the head, and the upstream printed shell holds it all. This package covers the servo wiring and its separate 5 V supply, a small printed power hub, and a servo centring tool; the robot's behaviour comes from the upstream Stack-chan firmware.
