Unplug USB and disconnect any battery before you solder or change a wire. Work on a surface that does not burn, and wash your hands after handling solder.

Before you switch it on

  1. Look. Every joint is a shiny cone. No solder touches a neighbouring pad. No stray strands of wire.
  2. These must beep. Probe the two ends of every connection.
  3. Plug in. Nothing gets warm, nothing smells. If something does, unplug at once and go back to step 2.
  4. Flash the bring-up test. It reports every part it can find before you load the real firmware.pio run -e bringup -t upload && pio device monitor

Tikk is built

Tikk is a Casio F-91W with its circuit board swapped for a Sensor Watch Pro: same case, same LCD, same three buttons, but the watch faces are open-source C you choose and compile yourself. It is for someone who wants a programmable watch that still lasts a year on a coin cell. Nothing is soldered: the board drops into the Casio's own frame.
