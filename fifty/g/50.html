Before you switch it on

  1. Look. Every joint is a shiny cone. No solder touches a neighbouring pad. No stray strands of wire.
  2. These must not beep. Set the multimeter to continuity and probe each pair. A beep means a short: find it before you plug in.
    3V3GND+5VGND
  3. These must beep. Probe the two ends of every connection.
    GNDDisplay GND · Bitaxe Gamma 601 DGND
    3V3Display VCC · Bitaxe Gamma 601 3V3
    I2C_SCLDisplay SCK · Bitaxe Gamma 601 SCL
    I2C_SDADisplay SDA · Bitaxe Gamma 601 SDA
    +5V5 V supply +5V · Bitaxe Gamma 601 5V
    GND5 V supply GND · Bitaxe Gamma 601 GND
  4. Plug in. Nothing gets warm, nothing smells. If something does, unplug at once and go back to step 2.
  5. Flash the bring-up test. It reports every part it can find before you load the real firmware.pio run -e bringup -t upload && pio device monitor

Øks is built

Øks is an assembled Bitaxe Gamma 601 open-hardware miner taken through a careful bring-up: a quiet Noctua fan, the strip display moved to a lid window, the 5 V 6 A supply from the box and a ventilated printed case. A read-only bench sketch checks the power regulator, temperature sensor and fan before the upstream ESP-Miner firmware goes back on. It is an electronics project for learning ASIC power, thermals and telemetry, not a way to earn money.
