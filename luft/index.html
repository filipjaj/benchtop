How it works.

Luft sits on your desk and shows temperature, humidity and air pressure on a small OLED. Press the button to cycle through readings and a 24 hour trend. It runs from any USB charger and keeps its data on the device.

1

Plug it in

Any USB charger or laptop port powers it. It starts measuring as soon as it has power.

2

Glance at it

The current temperature is always on screen, with humidity and pressure underneath.

3

Press for history

One button cycles through the last 24 hours as a simple trend line.

Questions, answered.

Do I need to have soldered before?

No. The guide starts with how to make a good joint and what a bad one looks like, and every connection is drawn with a close-up of the exact pad.

Can I build it without a 3D printer?

Yes. It works on the bench without the case, and print services accept the included 3MF file as it is.

Which USB cable does it need?

A USB-C cable that carries data, for the first flash. After that any USB-C charger cable will do.

Can I change what it shows?

Yes. The firmware is a small PlatformIO project, and the pin numbers are generated from the wiring so they cannot drift apart.

Has anyone built this one?

Not yet. The wiring is checked by software and the case is generated from the part sizes, but nobody has assembled this exact kit. Treat the first build as the test, and measure your parts before printing.
